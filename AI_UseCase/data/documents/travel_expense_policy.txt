ACME CORPORATION - TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY
Version 1.8 | Effective Date: February 1, 2025

============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This policy outlines the guidelines for business travel and expense reimbursement at ACME Corporation. It ensures consistent, fair, and fiscally responsible handling of travel-related expenses.

============================================================
2. SCOPE
============================================================

This policy applies to all ACME employees, contractors, and approved third parties traveling on company business.

============================================================
3. TRAVEL APPROVAL
============================================================

3.1 Pre-Approval Requirements
All business travel must be pre-approved:
- Domestic travel: Manager approval required
- International travel: VP-level approval required
- Travel exceeding $5,000: CFO approval required

3.2 Booking Process
- All travel must be booked through the approved travel portal (TravelHub)
- Bookings should be made at least 14 days in advance when possible
- Last-minute bookings (under 7 days) require manager justification

============================================================
4. TRANSPORTATION
============================================================

4.1 Air Travel
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Premium economy for flights 6-10 hours
- Business class for flights over 10 hours (VP approval required)
- Book lowest logical fare (within 2 hours of preferred departure)
- Personal frequent flyer miles may be retained by the employee

4.2 Ground Transportation
- Rental cars: Compact/mid-size for individuals, full-size for groups of 3+
- Rideshare (Uber/Lyft): Permitted for business purposes
- Personal vehicle: Reimbursed at IRS standard mileage rate ($0.67/mile for 2025)
- Parking: Actual costs reimbursed with receipts

4.3 Rail Travel
- Standard class for trips under 3 hours
- Business class for trips over 3 hours
- First class requires VP approval

============================================================
5. ACCOMMODATION
============================================================

5.1 Hotel Policy
- Maximum nightly rates (excluding taxes):
  - Tier 1 cities (NYC, SF, London, Tokyo): $250/night
  - Tier 2 cities (Chicago, Boston, Berlin): $200/night
  - All other locations: $150/night
- Extended stays (5+ nights): Corporate rate or Airbnb permitted
- Room type: Standard single room

5.2 Exceptions
- Rates exceeding limits require manager pre-approval
- Conference hotel rates are automatically approved if booked through the event
- Safety considerations may justify higher-rated accommodations

============================================================
6. MEALS & ENTERTAINMENT
============================================================

6.1 Daily Meal Allowance (Per Diem)
- Domestic travel:
  - Breakfast: $15
  - Lunch: $25
  - Dinner: $40
  - Total daily maximum: $80
- International travel:
  - Varies by country; refer to the international per diem schedule on TravelHub

6.2 Client Entertainment
- Pre-approval required for expenses exceeding $100/person
- Maximum: $150/person for client dinners
- Must document: attendees, business purpose, company/organization
- Alcohol is limited to reasonable amounts during client meals

6.3 Team Meals
- Manager may approve team meals up to $50/person for team events
- Holiday/celebration meals: $75/person with director approval

============================================================
7. EXPENSE REPORTING
============================================================

7.1 Submission Requirements
- Submit expense reports within 30 days of travel completion
- Use the ExpenseTrack system for all submissions
- Original receipts required for expenses over $25
- Credit card statements are NOT acceptable substitutes for receipts

7.2 Required Documentation
Each expense must include:
- Date of expense
- Business purpose
- Names of attendees (for meals/entertainment)
- Original itemized receipt
- Currency conversion rate (for international expenses)

7.3 Processing Timeline
- Reports submitted by Friday → processed the following Wednesday
- Direct deposit reimbursement within 5 business days of approval
- Manager must approve within 7 days of submission

============================================================
8. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
============================================================

The following expenses will NOT be reimbursed:
- Personal entertainment (movies, personal sightseeing)
- Mini-bar charges
- Laundry (unless trip exceeds 5 consecutive days)
- First-class upgrades without approval
- Traffic violations, parking tickets, or towing fees
- Pet care or house-sitting
- Travel insurance (company provides coverage)
- Expenses for travel companions/family members
- Premium app subscriptions for personal use
- Gift shop purchases
- Gym access fees at hotels (unless part of room rate)

============================================================
9. TRAVEL SAFETY
============================================================

9.1 Travel Insurance
ACME provides comprehensive travel insurance for all business travel, including:
- Medical emergency coverage
- Trip cancellation/interruption
- Lost/stolen baggage
- Emergency evacuation

9.2 International Travel Safety
- Register with the company's travel security provider before international trips
- Check travel advisories and obtain required vaccinations
- Keep copies of passport and important documents
- Share itinerary with your manager and the travel team

9.3 Risk Areas
- Travel to high-risk countries requires Security team approval
- The company reserves the right to restrict travel to certain destinations
- Emergency contact: Global Security Operations Center (GSOC) at (555) 999-0000

============================================================
10. CORPORATE CREDIT CARD
============================================================

10.1 Eligibility
- Available to employees who travel more than 4 times per year
- Apply through Finance department

10.2 Usage Rules
- Use ONLY for business expenses
- Personal charges are strictly prohibited
- Report lost/stolen cards immediately to Finance
- Monthly statements must be reconciled within 15 days

10.3 Spending Limits
- Individual transaction: $5,000
- Monthly limit: $10,000
- Higher limits require Finance approval

============================================================
11. POLICY VIOLATIONS
============================================================

Violations of this travel and expense policy may result in:
- Denial of expense reimbursement
- Revocation of corporate credit card
- Disciplinary action as outlined in the Employee Handbook
- Requirement to repay unauthorized expenses
