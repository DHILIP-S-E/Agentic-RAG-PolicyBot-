ACME CORPORATION - DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY POLICY
Version 2.1 | Effective Date: March 1, 2025

============================================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
============================================================

This policy establishes the framework for data privacy and security practices at ACME Corporation. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers who handle personal data or confidential business information.

============================================================
2. DATA CLASSIFICATION
============================================================

2.1 Public Data
Information that is freely available to the public.
Examples: Marketing materials, public press releases, job postings.
Handling: No special restrictions.

2.2 Internal Data
Information intended for use within ACME only.
Examples: Internal memos, org charts, project plans, meeting notes.
Handling: Should not be shared externally without authorization.

2.3 Confidential Data
Sensitive business information requiring protection.
Examples: Financial reports, strategic plans, vendor contracts, source code.
Handling: Encrypted storage, access restricted to authorized personnel.

2.4 Restricted Data
Highly sensitive data with legal or regulatory requirements.
Examples: Personal Identifiable Information (PII), health records, payment card data, trade secrets.
Handling: Strong encryption required, strict access controls, audit logging mandatory.

============================================================
3. PERSONAL DATA HANDLING
============================================================

3.1 Collection Principles
- Collect only data necessary for the stated purpose (data minimization)
- Obtain explicit consent where required
- Provide clear privacy notices explaining data use
- Document the legal basis for processing

3.2 Employee Data
ACME collects and processes employee personal data including:
- Name, address, contact information
- Social Security Number and tax information
- Banking details for payroll
- Performance records
- Health information (insurance purposes only)

This data is used solely for employment purposes and is retained according to our retention schedule.

3.3 Customer Data
Customer data must be:
- Processed only for the purposes outlined in our customer agreements
- Stored in approved systems with appropriate security controls
- Not transferred to third countries without adequate safeguards
- Deleted or anonymized when no longer needed

3.4 Data Subject Rights
Individuals have the right to:
- Access their personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of their data ("right to be forgotten")
- Restrict processing of their data
- Data portability (receive data in a machine-readable format)
- Object to automated decision-making

Requests should be submitted to privacy@acmecorp.com and will be processed within 30 days.

============================================================
4. DATA SECURITY MEASURES
============================================================

4.1 Access Controls
- Role-based access control (RBAC) for all systems
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) required for all accounts
- Principle of least privilege applied
- Access reviews conducted quarterly
- Immediate access revocation upon employee departure

4.2 Encryption Standards
- Data at rest: AES-256 encryption
- Data in transit: TLS 1.2 or higher
- Email: S/MIME or PGP for sensitive communications
- Portable devices: Full disk encryption required

4.3 Password Policy
- Minimum 12 characters
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
- Changed every 90 days
- No reuse of last 12 passwords
- Account lockout after 5 failed attempts

4.4 Network Security
- Firewall protection on all network boundaries
- Intrusion detection/prevention systems (IDS/IPS)
- VPN required for remote access to internal systems
- Network segmentation between departments
- Regular vulnerability scanning and penetration testing

4.5 Physical Security
- Badge access for all ACME facilities
- Visitor sign-in and escort requirements
- CCTV monitoring at entry points
- Secure disposal of physical documents (cross-cut shredding)
- Clean desk policy for all workstations

============================================================
5. GDPR COMPLIANCE
============================================================

5.1 Applicability
ACME complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for all processing of personal data related to individuals in the European Economic Area (EEA).

5.2 Data Protection Officer
ACME has appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO):
Name: Jane Smith
Email: dpo@acmecorp.com
Phone: (555) 987-6543

5.3 Legal Bases for Processing
ACME processes personal data based on:
- Consent: Where the individual has given clear consent
- Contract: Necessary for performing a contract with the individual
- Legal Obligation: Required by law
- Legitimate Interest: For legitimate business purposes, balanced against individual rights

5.4 International Data Transfers
Personal data transferred outside the EEA must have:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) in place
- Adequacy decision from the European Commission, OR
- Binding Corporate Rules approved by supervisory authority

5.5 Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA)
A DPIA must be conducted before:
- Implementing new processing activities involving personal data
- Introducing new technology that processes personal data
- Large-scale processing of sensitive data
- Systematic monitoring of individuals

============================================================
6. DATA BREACH RESPONSE
============================================================

6.1 Definition
A data breach is any unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of personal or confidential data.

6.2 Reporting
All suspected data breaches must be reported immediately to:
- Information Security Team: security@acmecorp.com
- Security Hotline: (555) 111-2222

6.3 Response Timeline
- Hour 0-1: Initial containment and assessment
- Hour 1-4: Incident response team assembled
- Hour 4-24: Full investigation and scope determination
- Within 72 hours: Notification to supervisory authority (GDPR requirement)
- Without undue delay: Notification to affected individuals (if high risk)

6.4 Breach Response Team
- Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) - Lead
- Data Protection Officer
- Legal Counsel
- Communications/PR
- Affected Business Unit Head

============================================================
7. THIRD-PARTY DATA PROCESSING
============================================================

7.1 Vendor Assessment
All third-party vendors who process data on ACME's behalf must:
- Complete a security questionnaire
- Provide SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certification
- Sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- Undergo annual security reviews

7.2 Sub-Processing
Vendors must obtain ACME's approval before engaging sub-processors. The same data protection standards apply to all sub-processors.

============================================================
8. DATA RETENTION AND DISPOSAL
============================================================

8.1 Retention Schedule
- Employee records: 7 years after termination
- Customer data: Duration of contract + 5 years
- Financial records: 7 years
- Marketing consent records: Duration of consent + 3 years
- System logs: 1 year
- CCTV footage: 30 days

8.2 Disposal Methods
- Electronic data: Cryptographic erasure or secure overwrite (DoD 5220.22-M)
- Paper documents: Cross-cut shredding
- Storage media: Degaussing or physical destruction
- Disposal must be documented and certified

============================================================
9. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES
============================================================

All employees must:
- Complete annual data privacy training
- Report suspected data breaches immediately
- Lock workstations when unattended
- Use approved tools and systems for data processing
- Not store company data on personal devices without approval
- Follow the clean desk policy
- Use encrypted channels for sensitive communications

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.
