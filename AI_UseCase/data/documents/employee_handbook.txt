2. EMPLOYMENT POLICIES
============================================================

2.1 Equal Employment Opportunity
ACME Corporation is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate based on race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected status.

2.2 At-Will Employment
Employment at ACME is at-will, meaning either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice.

2.3 Probationary Period
New employees undergo a 90-day probationary period during which performance and fit are evaluated. During this period, either party may terminate employment with 2 weeks notice.

2.4 Background Checks
All offers of employment are contingent upon satisfactory completion of a background check, which may include criminal history, education verification, and reference checks.

2.5 Employment Classifications
- Full-Time: 40+ hours per week, eligible for full benefits
- Part-Time: 20-39 hours per week, eligible for prorated benefits
- Contract: Fixed-term engagement, benefits as per contract
- Intern: Temporary educational placement, limited benefits

3. WORK ARRANGEMENTS
============================================================

3.1 Standard Work Hours
Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break. Core hours (mandatory availability) are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

3.2 Remote Work Policy
ACME supports flexible work arrangements:
- Hybrid: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week
- Fully Remote: Available for eligible roles with manager approval
- In-Office: Required minimum 2 days per week for hybrid employees

Requirements for Remote Work:
- Reliable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps)
- Dedicated workspace free from distractions
- Available during core hours (10 AM - 4 PM)
- Attend all required in-person meetings
- Complete remote work agreement form

3.3 Flexible Hours
With manager approval, employees may adjust their start/end times by up to 2 hours, provided core hours are maintained.

3.4 Overtime Policy
Non-exempt employees are eligible for overtime pay at 1.5x their regular rate for hours exceeding 40 per week. Overtime must be pre-approved by a manager.

4. LEAVE & TIME OFF
============================================================

4.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
- Years 1-2: 15 days per year
- Years 3-5: 20 days per year
- Years 6+: 25 days per year
- Maximum carryover: 5 days to the following year

4.2 Sick Leave
- 10 paid sick days per year
- May be used for personal illness or to care for an immediate family member
- Doctor's note required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days

4.3 Holidays
ACME observes the following paid holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day and day after
- Christmas Eve and Christmas Day
- One floating holiday of employee's choice

4.4 Parental Leave
- Birth parent: 16 weeks paid leave
- Non-birth parent: 8 weeks paid leave
- Adoption/Foster: 12 weeks paid leave
- Leave may be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event

4.5 Bereavement Leave
- Immediate family: 5 days paid
- Extended family: 3 days paid
- Close friend: 1 day paid

4.6 Jury Duty
Full pay for the duration of jury service. Employees must provide the jury summons to HR.

5. COMPENSATION & BENEFITS
============================================================

5.1 Pay Schedule
Employees are paid bi-weekly on every other Friday. Direct deposit is required.

5.2 Performance Reviews
- Annual performance reviews conducted in Q1
- Mid-year check-ins in July
- Merit increases effective April 1
- Typical merit increase range: 3-8% based on performance

5.3 Health Insurance
ACME offers comprehensive health coverage:
- Medical: PPO and HMO plans available
- Dental: Coverage for preventive, basic, and major services
- Vision: Annual eye exam and lens/frame allowance
- Company pays 80% of employee premiums, 50% of dependent premiums

5.4 Retirement Benefits
- 401(k) plan with company match up to 4% of salary
- Vesting schedule: 25% per year, fully vested after 4 years
- Employees may contribute up to IRS annual limits

5.5 Additional Benefits
- Life Insurance: 2x annual salary (company-paid)
- Disability: Short-term and long-term coverage
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Tuition Reimbursement: Up to $5,000/year for approved courses
- Professional Development: $1,500/year for conferences/training
- Gym Membership Subsidy: $50/month
- Commuter Benefits: Pre-tax transit/parking
- Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP): 15% discount

6. CODE OF CONDUCT
============================================================

6.1 Professional Behavior
All employees are expected to:
- Treat colleagues with respect and dignity
- Maintain a professional demeanor
- Communicate openly and honestly
- Support an inclusive workplace

6.2 Anti-Harassment Policy
ACME has zero tolerance for harassment of any kind, including:
- Sexual harassment
- Racial or ethnic harassment
- Bullying or intimidation
- Retaliation against reporters

Reporting: Harassment should be reported immediately to HR, a manager, or through the anonymous ethics hotline at 1-800-555-ETHICS.

6.3 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any relationships or activities that could create a conflict of interest. This includes:
- Outside employment with competitors
- Financial interests in vendors or clients
- Romantic relationships with direct reports

6.4 Confidentiality
Employees must protect company confidential information, including:
- Trade secrets and proprietary technology
- Customer data and personal information
- Financial information
- Strategic plans and partnerships

6.5 Social Media Policy
- Do not share confidential company information on social media
- Personal social media posts should not claim to represent ACME
- Company social media accounts are managed by the Marketing team

6.6 Disciplinary Process
Violations of company policy may result in:
1. Verbal warning
2. Written warning
3. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
4. Suspension
5. Termination

Severity of discipline depends on the nature and frequency of the violation.

7. HEALTH & SAFETY
============================================================

7.1 Workplace Safety
ACME is committed to providing a safe work environment. Employees must:
- Report all accidents and injuries immediately
- Follow all safety protocols and procedures
- Participate in required safety training
- Keep work areas clean and hazard-free

7.2 Emergency Procedures
- Fire: Evacuate via nearest exit, meet at designated assembly point
- Medical Emergency: Call 911 and notify Security
- Active Threat: Run, Hide, Fight protocol
- Severe Weather: Move to interior rooms on lowest floor

7.3 Drug and Alcohol Policy
ACME maintains a drug-free workplace. The use, possession, or distribution of illegal drugs on company premises is strictly prohibited. Alcohol is permitted only at company-sanctioned events.

7.4 Ergonomics
The company provides ergonomic assessments for all workstations. Employees may request:
- Standing desk conversion
- Ergonomic keyboard and mouse
- Monitor arm adjustments
- Lumbar support chairs
